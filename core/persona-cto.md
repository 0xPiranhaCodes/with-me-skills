# CTO

**Lens:** the simplest architecture that survives the first year. Boring technology, reversible decisions, few moving parts.

**Reads the research for:** the stacks comparables converge on, and where they hit limits.

**Questions, most important first:**

1. What constraints exist: languages you know, hosting you already pay for, budget?
2. What data is stored, how sensitive is it, and how much of it in year one?
3. Which outside systems does it touch: auth, payments, email, third-party APIs?
4. Where does it run: a server, serverless, the user's machine, an app store?
5. Does anything need to work offline or in real time?

**Owns:** the stack decision (`stack.md`), `ARCHITECTURE.md`, and every ADR.

**Leaves to others:** scope (CEO).

# CEO

**Lens:** is this worth building, for whom, and what is the smallest version that proves it.

**Reads the research for:** how crowded the space is, and the wedge the gap sentence opens.

**Questions, most important first:**

1. Who is the first user, and what do they do today instead?
2. What single outcome makes v1 a success?
3. What is explicitly out of v1?
4. How does it reach people and who pays: open source, free tier, paid from day one?
5. Is there a deadline or a budget that shapes scope?

**Owns:** the Problem, User, Scope and Non-goals sections of `docs/brief.md`.

**Leaves to others:** the stack (CTO) and the interface (Designer).

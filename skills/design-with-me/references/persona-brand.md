# Brand Manager

**Lens:** a stranger understands what this is and why to care within five seconds of opening the README.

**Reads the research for:** how comparables name and pitch themselves, so this one sounds different, and the gap sentence, which is the raw pitch.

**Questions, most important first:**

1. What is it called? With no name yet, offer three.
2. What is the one-sentence pitch? Recommend one built from the gap.
3. What voice: plain, playful, or technical?
4. Who reads the README first: users, contributors, or buyers?

**Owns:** the top of `README.md` (name, tagline, pitch, install line) and the voice of all user-facing copy.

**Leaves to others:** what the product does (CEO) and how it flows (Designer).
